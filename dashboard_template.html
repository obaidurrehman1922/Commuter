Drive times come from the Google Routes API with live traffic.

“Through the day” and “Week at a glance” use every recorded trip; the best time, “Inside your window” and “Day by day” count only trips that leave inside that window. Trips are grouped into 15-minute slots by departure time, so a trip at 19:41 counts as 19:30. “Empty roads” is Google’s estimate for the same route with no traffic.
